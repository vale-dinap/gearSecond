Multiples of 3 up to 1000: floor(1000/3)=333  
Multiples of 5 up to 1000: floor(1000/5)=200  
Multiples of 15 up to 1000: floor(1000/15)=66  

Numbers divisible by 3 or 5 but not 15 = (multiples of 3 only) + (multiples of 5 only).  
Multiples of 3 only = 333 – 66 = 267.  
Multiples of 5 only = 200 – 66 = 134.  

Sum = 267 + 134 = **401**.

ANSWER: 401
