Derangements of \(n\) elements satisfy  
\(D_n=(n-1)(D_{\,n-1}+D_{\,n-2})\).

Start:
\(D_1=0,\; D_2=1.\)

Compute sequentially:

* \(D_3=2(D_2+D_1)=2(1+0)=2\)
* \(D_4=3(D_3+D_2)=3(2+1)=9\)
* \(D_5=4(D_4+D_3)=4(9+2)=44\)
* \(D_6=5(D_5+D_4)=5(44+9)=5·53=265\)

Thus 265 permutations of \(\{1,2,3,4,5,6\}\) have no element in its original position.

ANSWER: 265
