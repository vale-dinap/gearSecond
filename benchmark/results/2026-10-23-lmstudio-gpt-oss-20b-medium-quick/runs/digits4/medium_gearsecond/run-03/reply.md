Count solutions to \(a+b+c+d=10\) with \(a\in[1,9],\,b,c,d\in[0,9]\).  
Set \(x=a-1\) (\(x\ge0,\ x\le8\)). Then  

\(x+b+c+d = 9\).

Number of non‑negative solutions to \(x+b+c+d=9\): \(\binom{12}{3}=220\).

The only forbidden case is \(x=9\) (which would give \(a=10\)), giving one solution.  
Thus valid solutions: \(220-1 = 219\).

**ANSWER:** 219
