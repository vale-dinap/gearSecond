The \(8\)-step self-avoiding walks are equidistributed among the four open quadrants and among the four axial rays by the rotational symmetry of the square lattice. Consequently, if \(T\) is the total number of \(8\)-step walks and \(A\) is the number that terminate on the positive horizontal axis, the desired count is \((T-4A)/4\).

The total is the classical value \(T=5916\). The axial count \(A\) is obtained by enumerating the possible step multisets (determined by the requirement that the net vertical displacement vanish) and, for each multiset, counting the self-avoiding realisations:

- \(1\) walk ends at \((8,0)\),
- \(42\) walks end at \((6,0)\),
- \(126\) walks end at \((4,0)\),
- \(92\) walks end at \((2,0)\).

(The last two figures arise by separating the west-free walks, which are ordinary distributions of pure vertical runs into the columns, from the walks that contain one or more west steps; the latter are classified by the unique gap that contains each west step and are retained only when the horizontal intervals lying on each fixed horizontal line are pairwise disjoint.) Hence \(A=261\).

It follows that the number of walks ending in the open first quadrant is
\[
(5916-4\cdot261)/4=1218.
